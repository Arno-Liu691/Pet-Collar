This dataset contains three motion conditions. Approximately 69–100 s is sustained low-amplitude body sway: the algorithm should remain in REST_MEASUREMENT and continue estimating HR and RR whenever the signals remain reliable. Approximately 155–158 s is moderate motion: it should be treated as vital-signal contamination, reducing SQI or temporarily suppressing valid HR/RR output without necessarily leaving the resting state. Approximately 201–203 s is severe motion: the algorithm must immediately exit REST_MEASUREMENT and reset the HR/RR estimator.

For initial PC-based analysis, candidate thresholds may include: stable motion with acceleration-magnitude range below approximately 0.03 g, gyro p95 below approximately 8 dps, and jerk p95 below approximately 1.2 g/s; moderate motion with acceleration range around 0.25–1.0 g or jerk p95 around 3.5–15 g/s; and severe motion with acceleration range at or above approximately 2.0 g, gyro p95 at or above approximately 150–200 dps, or jerk p95 at or above approximately 30 g/s. These values are provisional search ranges only. Use combinations of features, motion duration, and observed HR/RR contamination rather than converting any single value into a fixed rule, and validate all thresholds on additional human recordings and later canine data to avoid overfitting.
